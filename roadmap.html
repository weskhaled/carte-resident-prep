📬 After the exams — the renewal application itself

Timeline: results arrive 2–4 weeks after the TCF/TEF; the civic attestation is usually faster. You'll file the renewal on ANEF in the window your préfecture allows (typically between 4 and 2 months before expiry — for the expiry date set on the dashboard, that's November 2026 → January 2027).

Nationality note: if you are Tunisian, the accord franco-tunisien of 17 March 1988 governs several 10-year-card categories (spouse of a French national, parent of a French child…). Depending on your category, some conditions can differ from the general CESEDA rules. Ask your préfecture exactly which list of documents applies to your case — but preparing both exams is the safe play either way.
