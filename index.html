The two requirements

🏛️ Test civique mandatory

40 QCM on computer · 45 min · pass at 32/40 (80%) · 28 knowledge + 12 situational questions · ~30–90 € · attestation valid forever.

Themes: values of the Republic, institutions, rights & duties, history/geography/culture, daily life in France.

Start a mock exam Read the guide & fiches

🇫🇷 French level B1 mandatory

Proven by TCF or TEF (the only accepted tests, ~1h30, 135–210 €, valid 2 years) — or by a French diploma ≥ B1 (DELF B1, CAP/bac/licence obtained in France…) which exempts you from the test.

4 skills tested: listening, reading, writing, speaking.
